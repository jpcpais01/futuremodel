How it is kept

A forward paper record. Each decision is written to a hash-chained ledger and the index enters it at that trading day's open (9:30 New York), with a 0.1% cost.

There is no backtest on purpose: a language model already knows the past, so only the record from here forward counts. Judge it after several months.

About

Two paper indexes. Core v1.0 is a hand-built book with fixed rotation rules. Council is a council of Claude agents that meets every Monday before the US open and holds what it judges the best portfolio.

Each decision is bought at the 9:30 New York open of its day, with 0.1% per trade, and compared with SPY and QQQ bought at the same open.

Where the numbers come from

GitHub updates both indexes every hour while New York trades and once after the close, and keeps them in the futuremodel repository. This app and the PC app only read them, so there is nothing to set up.
