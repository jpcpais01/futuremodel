# The Future · Oazy Research

**Awareness:** The future is where the future is. We need to be wise ofc, but we can't ignore the fact that whatever is in the future, is what is worth holding.

Marked on free daily closes, after 0.1% per trade, against SPY and QQQ bought at the same moment.
